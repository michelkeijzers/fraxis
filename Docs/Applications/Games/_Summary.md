## Ideas

### 1. Checkers (Dammen) on a $5 \times 20$ Board
* **Concept:** A custom variant of checkers adapted to fit the dimensions of your WS2812B LED strips ($5$ rows wide).
* **Gameplay:** Players use the Atari 2600 or Arduino joysticks to navigate pieces across the $5 \times 20$ grid, moving lengthwise across the board. TM1637 and LCD displays track turns, captured pieces, and timers.

### 2. Ludo (Mens erger je niet) on a Matrix
* **Concept:** A classic multi-player board game adapted for a matrix layout.
* **Gameplay:** Supports 4 players on a $5 \times 72$ pixel layout. Players use joysticks to roll dice virtually and move their pawns across the long LED strip track.

### 3. Go (Scrolling Viewport Variant)
* **Concept:** Traditional Go ($13 \times 13$ or $19 \times 19$) played using a localized scrolling "viewport" (such as $5 \times 13$ or $5 \times 19$) on the narrow $5 \times 72$ physical matrix.
* **Gameplay:** Players scroll their camera perspective across the larger virtual board using joysticks, introducing a tactical "Fog of War" exploration element to the game.

### 4. Gomoku / Pente (Five-in-a-Row)
* **Concept:** A simpler alternative to Go played on similar grids where the goal is to align five pieces in a row without complex liberty or Ko calculations.

### 5. Tron Light Cycles
* **Concept:** Fast-paced arcade action where players drive light cycles leaving a trailing pixel path behind them.
* **Gameplay:** Perfect for a $5 \times 72$ horizontal layout, where hitting a wall or a light trail results in instant elimination.

### 6. Side-Scrolling Space Shooter
* **Concept:** A retro arcade shooter where your ship moves vertically on the left side of the $5 \times 72$ matrix while enemies and asteroids approach from the right.

### 7. Line-Runner / Copter Game
* **Concept:** An endurance reflex game where you guide a single pixel through a scrolling cave or tunnel, avoiding upper and lower boundaries.

### 8. Snake Race
* **Concept:** A multiplayer or single-player variation of Snake optimized for the long horizontal display using retro 5-direction joysticks.

### 9. Lane Defender / Tower Defense
* **Concept:** Enemies march from right to left across the 5 distinct rows (lanes) of the matrix toward your base, and you must shoot or place defenses to stop them.

### 10. Space Invaders LED
* **Concept:** Classic vertical/horizontal invasion shooter. Wave of alien pixels shift back and forth, descending towards the player cannon defense line.

### 11. Asteroid Belt Evasion
* **Concept:** Navigate a spaceship through a dense asteroid field where boulder clusters split into smaller pixel fragments upon impact.

### 12. Frog River Crossing
* **Concept:** Guide a frog pixel safely from the bottom/left row across moving logs and hazard lanes to reach safety zones on the far side.

### 13. Breakout Matrix / Arkanoid
* **Concept:** Deflect a ball off a moveable paddle to destroy rows of brick pixels on the opposite end of the playfield.

### 14. Pac-Man Dot Chaser
* **Concept:** Navigate a maze layout (or scrolling single corridor) collecting power pellets while dodging ghost pixels programmed with distinct AI behaviors.

### 15. Dino Hop (Endless Runner)

### 16. Missile Command Defense
* **Concept:** Incoming ICBM warheads descend toward target cities across the 72-column grid; player aims anti-missile intercept explosions.

### 17. Whack-A-Pixel

### 18. Flappy Pixel

### 19. Tug-of-War Rope Push

### 20. Slalom Ski Racer

## Ideas

## Firework (Particle Physics Simulator)
Concept: A pure simulation loop tracking gravity, velocity, and particle lifespans.

Visualization: Random or joystick-triggered fireworks bursting across the top of the matrix, with glowing sparks raining down and fading out into the dark background.

### Game of Life
Concept: A zero-player mathematical simulation governed by simple rules of birth, survival, and death.

Visualization: Complex organic cell colonies evolve, expand, and die out across the matrix grid. You can include different color rules (e.g., age-based coloring where older cells shift from blue to red) and joystick controls to "seed" new pixel clusters manually.

## Liquid Simulator / Cellular Lava Lamp
Concept: A 2D metaball or fluid simulation scaled down to the matrix resolution.

Visualization: Soft, glowing blobs of blended colors that slowly drift, merge, and bounce off the edges of the screen, creating a relaxing ambient lava lamp effect.
